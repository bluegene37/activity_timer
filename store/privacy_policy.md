# Privacy Policy for Activity Timer

**Effective Date:** September 20, 2026  
**Last Updated:** September 20, 2026  

This Privacy Policy describes how **Activity Timer** (Package: `dev.genexis.activitytimer`), developed by **Gene Ray Medel** ("we", "us", or "our"), handles your information when you use our mobile application.

---

### 1. Summary — Zero Personal Data Collection

We believe privacy is a fundamental right. **Activity Timer collects, transmits, sells, shares, or tracks zero personal data.**

- **No User Accounts:** You do not need to register, create an profile, or log in to use this app.
- **No Analytics / Telemetry:** We do not track how you use the app, which timers you run, or how often you open it.
- **No Third-Party Advertising:** The application contains zero advertisements, trackers, SDKs, or marketing tools.
- **No Network Requests:** Activity Timer functions entirely offline. It does not contact external servers or sync data over the internet.

---

### 2. Information Stored Locally on Your Device

All routines, intervals, recipes, and preferences you configure are stored **strictly and solely on your physical device**:

- **Custom Activities & Multi-Step Routines**
- **Interval Timers & HIIT / Tabata Configurations**
- **Coffee Pour-Over Recipes & Pour Schedules**
- **App Preferences** (theme modes, sound alerts, wake-lock behavior, display preferences)

This data is maintained locally using encrypted/embedded on-device storage (`Hive CE`). It never leaves your device and is deleted if you uninstall the app or clear app storage.

---

### 3. Device Permissions & System Capabilities

Activity Timer requests only the minimum permissions necessary to deliver essential timer and alarm functionality:

- **Post Notifications (`POST_NOTIFICATIONS`):** Delivers a notification when an activity, interval period, or pour-over phase completes while the app is in the background.
- **Exact Alarms (`USE_EXACT_ALARM`, `SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM`):** Allows timer alerts to ring at the exact scheduled second without operating system throttling or power-saving drift.
- **Vibration (`VIBRATE`):** Haptic and vibration feedback when timer milestones and completion alarms sound.
- **Wake Lock (`WAKELOCK`):** Keeps your display awake during an active timer or workout session when explicitly toggled on in settings.
- **Reboot Completed (`RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED`):** Restores any pending scheduled reminder alarms if the device reboots.

None of these permissions are used to collect, inspect, or transmit user information.

---

### 4. Third-Party Services

Activity Timer does not integrate any third-party tracking, crash reporting (e.g., Firebase Crashlytics, Sentry), or analytics services. No third parties have access to your data.

---

### 5. Children’s Privacy

Because Activity Timer collects zero personal information, it is safe for users of all ages and does not collect information from children under 13.
