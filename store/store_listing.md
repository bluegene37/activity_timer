## Full Description (Both Stores)

Take control of your time with Activity Timer — the versatile, privacy-focused offline timer designed for workouts, multi-step routines, productivity, and precision coffee brewing.

No subscriptions. No advertisements. No accounts. Every feature works completely offline from the moment you install.

---

### 🔥 INTERVAL TIMER (HIIT & WORKOUTS)
- Configure custom Work, Rest, and Prep intervals with round counters.
- Ideal for High-Intensity Interval Training (HIIT), Tabata, boxing rounds, calisthenics, and circuit training.
- Clean high-contrast display with instant color-coded visual cues (green for work, amber for rest).
- Background audio cues: listen to countdown beeps and completion chimes without staring at your screen.

---

### 📋 STEP-BY-STEP ACTIVITY ROUTINES
- Build multi-step routines with personalized names and custom durations for each stage.
- Perfect for morning habits, cooking recipes, yoga sequences, physiotherapy, stretching, and study sessions.
- Reorder steps effortlessly with drag-and-drop.
- Auto-advance or pause between steps depending on your preferred workflow.

---

### ☕ SPECIALTY COFFEE POUR-OVER TIMER
- Dial in your daily brew with a dedicated pour-over mode.
- Calculate exact brew ratios, coffee dose, and water targets in grams.
- Program bloom duration, multi-stage pour intervals, and drawdown times.
- Real-time pour rate guidance to master consistent extraction every morning.

---

### 🔊 AUDIO CUES & BACKGROUND ALARMS
- High-quality audio alert sounds designed to be heard over music or across the room.
- Runs reliably when your phone is locked or while multitasking in other apps.
- Optional screen wake-lock keeps the timer visible without auto-dimming.
- Haptic and vibration feedback for silent gym sessions.

---

### 🎨 REFINED, INTUITIVE DESIGN
- Elegant, minimalist interface that feels right at home on modern devices.
- Seamless Dark Mode and Light Mode support.
- Fast, fluid 120Hz animations with responsive controls.

---

### 🔒 100% PRIVATE & OFFLINE
- Activity Timer collects zero personal data, contains no trackers, and makes zero network requests.
- All routines, workouts, and settings are saved locally on your device.

The USE_EXACT_ALARM permission is critical to ensure that time-sensitive interval transitions and completion alerts ring at the exact second configured by the user, even when the device is in Doze or deep sleep mode."

## Google Play Console: Data Safety Declarations

- **Does your app collect or share any user data?**  
  Select **No**.
- **Is all user data encrypted in transit?**  
  Select **N/A** (app does not transfer data to external servers).
- **Does your app provide a way for users to request data deletion?**  
  Select **No** (data is stored only locally; uninstalling or clearing app data deletes all local files).
